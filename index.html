Welcome to Golden

A small hotel with a big welcome.

Golden Hotel is an independent 3-star hotel on Al Yanabii Street, renovated in 2022. Behind the marble lobby are 24 calm rooms and suites with velvet headboards, crisp white linen and room to unpack properly.

We’re not a big chain, and guests notice the difference. Read our reviews and you’ll see the same thing again and again: the staff made the stay.

How do I book?

Choose your room and dates above and tap “Send on WhatsApp”. Your request opens in WhatsApp with everything filled in. We reply with your price and confirm the booking. There’s no booking fee.

Do I pay in advance?

No. You pay at the hotel when you arrive. Bring cash to be safe and ask us on WhatsApp if you’d like to pay by card. Please bring photo ID; a deposit may be asked for at check-in.

Can I cancel?

Message us on WhatsApp before you confirm and we’ll explain the cancellation terms for your dates. If your plans change later, tell us as early as you can.

Is there parking?

Yes, free self-parking at the hotel for guests.

Can you pick me up from the airport?

Yes. We arrange paid transfers from Queen Alia International Airport, about 40 minutes away. Send us your flight number on WhatsApp.

Arriving late?

Check-in runs until midnight and the front desk is open 24 hours. If you land late, message us your arrival time and we’ll be ready for you.

Is the hotel accessible?

The entrance is stair-free, but the building has no elevator. If stairs are difficult, ask us for a lower floor and we’ll do our best.

Your room in Amman is one message away.

Tell us your dates and we’ll reply with your price and confirm your room. No booking fees, no payment until you arrive.
